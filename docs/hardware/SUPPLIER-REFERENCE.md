## Cost Optimization Tips

### 1. Consolidate Orders
- Combine all Adafruit/SparkFun items in one order (save on shipping)
- Use Mouser for all components (free shipping >$50)

### 2. Buy in Bulk
- RAKwireless: 10× RAK4631 gets 10% discount
- Mouser: 100× resistors cost $10 vs $10 for 10×
- JLCPCB: 30 boards barely costs more than 10

### 3. Substitute Parts
- Use generic JST connectors from AliExpress (1/3 price of Mouser)
- Generic 18650 batteries on Amazon vs. branded Panasonic
- DFRobot sensors (cheaper) vs. Adafruit (better docs)

### 4. Open-Source Alternatives
- Design own PCBs instead of buying breakout boards
- 3D print enclosures instead of buying injection-molded
- DIY antennas (1/4 wave wire) instead of commercial ($0 vs $5)
